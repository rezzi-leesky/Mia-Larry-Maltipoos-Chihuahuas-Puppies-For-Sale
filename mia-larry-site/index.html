About the breeds

Maltipoo

The Maltipoo is a cross between a Maltese and a Poodle. It is known for a soft, wavy coat and a friendly, people-loving nature.

Coat and adult size vary with each puppy’s parents, so please ask us about the puppy you are interested in.

Chihuahua

The Chihuahua is one of the smallest companion breeds. It is known for being loyal and bold, with a strong bond to its person.

Grooming needs depend on the coat type. Ask us about any Chihuahua puppy you are considering.

Both breeds are small and need regular veterinary care, daily attention and gentle training.

Frequently asked questions

Do Maltipoos shed?

Maltipoos typically shed very little, but the coat still needs regular brushing to prevent tangles. No dog is completely hypoallergenic, so spend time with the breed first if allergies are a concern.

Are small dogs good with children and other pets?

Many are, but small dogs are delicate. Young children should always be supervised around them, and introductions to other pets should be gradual.

How much exercise do they need?

Short daily walks and indoor play are enough for most small dogs.

Can they stay home alone?

These breeds bond closely with their people, so build up alone time gradually.

How often should I groom?

Brush regularly and plan professional grooming as your puppy’s coat requires. Ask us for guidance on the puppy you choose.

Can I meet my puppy before I commit?

Yes. We are happy to schedule a live video call and to share additional photos and videos.

Return & Refund Policy

We are committed to the lifelong well-being of every puppy we place. If, at any time, you are unable to keep your puppy, you agree to contact us before transferring ownership, rehoming, surrendering, or placing the puppy in a shelter.

Within 14 days of receiving your puppy, you may return the puppy if a licensed veterinarian documents a serious congenital or hereditary condition that significantly affects the puppy’s quality of life. Upon review of the veterinary documentation, we may offer a replacement puppy or a partial or full refund at our sole discretion.

Deposits are non-refundable because reserving a puppy removes it from availability to other families. However, if we are unable to provide the reserved puppy due to unforeseen circumstances, you may choose either a full refund of your deposit or transfer the deposit to another available puppy.

Returns based on personal circumstances, housing restrictions, allergies, lifestyle changes, financial reasons, or family decisions are not eligible for a refund. However, we will make reasonable efforts to assist with rehoming whenever possible.
